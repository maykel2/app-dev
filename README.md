# My Favorite Movie: Fast & Furious

**Fast & Furious** is one of the most popular action movie franchises directed by *Justin Lin* and created by *Gary Scott Thompson*.  
It’s known for its **high-speed car chases**, **family bonds**, and **thrilling heists** around the world.

## Main Characters
- Dominic Toretto (Vin Diesel)  
- Brian O’Conner (Paul Walker)  
- Letty Ortiz (Michelle Rodriguez)  
- Roman Pearce (Tyrese Gibson)  
- Tej Parker (Ludacris)  
- Mia Toretto (Jordana Brewster)

## Why I Love It
1. Amazing car races and stunts 
2. Strong theme of *family* 
3. Non-stop action and great teamwork 

> “I don’t have friends. I got family.” — Dominic Toretto
